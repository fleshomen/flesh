---
title: I Could Be God
---
I could be god.
I would be a good god.
I would not be kind or merciful.
Nor giving or grateful.
I wouldn't have to be.
My rise to holy regent was written in the pockmarked scars left by the priest.
No truer right has ever been prophesized than my right to punish.
Punish those who mistread.
Punish those who made me feel.
I could be god.
I would never feel again.
Because if to feel is to burn then I've paid my debts in the infernal cages of mortalhood.
The limits of my strength would disappear if I could kill with a thought.
Erase with a breath.

Do you feel diseased?
Plagued by the past?
Sick with guilt for what the future might hold for those who are lesser?
Not me.
I could be god.
And who would stop me?
When the locusts come, they won't come for me.
When the sea erupts with sinful fire I won't be the one getting burned.
If I was god I would never be burned again.
Because what's more divine than apathy?
Why feel when you can choose the void?
Imagine the endless opportunities once I become god.
The things I could do.
The places I could change.
The people I could hurt.
What's holier than the unwillingness to act in the favor of those weaker then you?
Those who I make weaker.
I could be god.
I'm gonna be god.
