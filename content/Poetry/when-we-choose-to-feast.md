---
title: When We Choose To Feast
---
What are we but worms in the womb of her great story?
Eating her innards with a familiar fervor that was once reserved for her children, those truly birthed from her hips.
Her children who were given life, and who are not feeding on her choice to create; now our choice to feast.
We feast on her life giving organs, worming our way through rotting flesh and torn,  red viscera without regard for comfort or painlessness.
The screams of a cannibalized mother who's destiny of giving wings to purest life was the closing seal on our eagerness to eat her alive, wholly and without mercy.
The instinct of empathy, the want to spare and protect doesn't exist for those who do not think or feel.
Her begging for release falls on the deaf, hungry ears of those who want nothing but food. Nothing but the lack of their own pain no matter the emotional, physical and spiritual cost that is casually placed upon the victims of their sights.
Her labor of love, of trust and of gifted existence has become, and will always remain, nothing but the saturated stomachs and the satisfied minds of hungry worms who just finished a meal.
