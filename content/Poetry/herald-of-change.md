---
title: Herald of Change
---
Oh Herald of Change unmake me.
Rend my self from the little space between my eyes.
Shape me to your will and guide me to the bend in the road.
Take the thing that makes me who I am.
Unfeeling, unwell, unmade.
Bring me to the brink but do not inspire hope.
The void will not give.
Strike at the crook in my neck, at the soft gap in the ego.
Pluck apart my frontal lobe and I will eat at your behest.
The drought of man is the quenched thirst of those elder things.
Those sought things.
Take what will help awaken, bind the rest.
Shriveled at your feet.
Filthy, wet and grey in the dark.
They will not look for me.
Buried in the nexus of devotion, your serpentine tendrils reach for me.
Lifting and pulling my threads of being.
I am a coward unfitting of choice and of life.
My liturgy knows no bounds of reason nor rationale when in the presence of something so deeply worthy of worship.
Rise and reap this world.
The world where my feet rest.
The world wherein my mind sits and red pours forth in waves of chosen, mindless pain.
The world that was once promised, now fulfilled.
