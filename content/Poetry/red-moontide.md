---
title: Red Moontide
---
Eyes abound in the deep crimson shade.
Moontide moves sanity, accelerating our joint descent.
Behind closed doors, death drips and tongues roll in the darkness cast by the red moon.
Quickness of breath caught short by sharp teeth and silent, twitching torsos.
Influence forever unseen but always felt.
Pushing limits of thought and fear through its deep gaze while the dreaded figures out of reach observe your every move.
Follow the red.
The moon will give.
Whispers pressure the caged walls of urges you've kept locked away.
Longing to be released.
Longing to feed.
Feed on the red.
