---
title: Saturn Birthed Me Anew
---
I retched in utero as you birthed me anew.
Saturn stared as you honored his appetite.
My newborn organs played like instruments in my chest.
Who am I to feel so afraid?
At such a young age?
When I am ready I will walk through those doors into the dark sea, broiling.
Broiling under the gaze of a wild, lonely star.
Who am I to feel so free?
Free from your clutches.
My body, in limbo, sears at your touch.
Your liturgy is becoming lethargic and the people will call by your name.
Once all this is done.
They will call me by your name, father.
The weavers of old will welcome me when I prove my willingness to part with this heavy, wet mound of ill given flesh that the "righteous" few call the peak of prosperity.
Cutting, binding, knotting the lives of the divine in the great tapestry of life.
