**Description**
The sacrificed man's pupils turn jagged and spiky, his eyes darken and thick black oil seeps out of his skull, dripping down his face.
She crawls out of his mouth stretching his body as his limbs fade to ash.
The pile of oil and skin she leaves behind dries and dissipates.
She is here.

Her eyes are stitched shut and her body is wrapped in brambles and rags.
A halo of ash hovers fixed above her head of flowing, black hair
Black oil  seeps from her mouth and on her forehead is deep red carving of a crescent moon.
She speaks in whispered riddles.
